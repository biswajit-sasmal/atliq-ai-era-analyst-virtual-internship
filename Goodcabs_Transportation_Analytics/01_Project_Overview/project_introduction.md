# Goodcabs | Transportation & Mobility Business Analytics

## 1. Project Overview

Goodcabs is a cab service company operating across 10 tier-2 cities in India. Its business model focuses on supporting local drivers by providing sustainable earning opportunities in their hometowns while delivering quality service to passengers.

This project analyzes Goodcabs' **H1 2026 historical data (January–June 2026)** to evaluate business performance against predefined targets and identify opportunities for operational improvement and sustainable growth.

The engagement is structured as a **Proof of Concept (POC)** to validate the analytical approach before integrating the dashboard with the company's live data pipeline.

## 2. Business Problem Statement

Goodcabs identified several business challenges:

- **City Performance:** Lack of visibility into which cities are outperforming or underperforming against business targets.
- **Passenger Retention:** Inconsistent Repeat Passenger Rates (RPR%) across cities, with limited understanding of the underlying factors.
- **Target Achievement:** Approximately 4 out of 10 cities are missing their monthly trip targets.
- **Passenger Acquisition vs. Retention:** Insufficient visibility into new and repeat passenger contributions, making marketing budget allocation difficult.

## 3. Project Objectives

The primary objectives are to:

1. Evaluate city-level and monthly operational performance against business targets.
2. Analyze new passenger acquisition and repeat passenger retention.
3. Identify variations in Repeat Passenger Rate across cities.
4. Assess trip volume, revenue, fare, and passenger performance.
5. Identify underperforming cities and recommend improvement opportunities.
6. Develop reusable SQL reports for monthly business monitoring.
7. Build an interactive Power BI dashboard for city-level performance tracking.
8. Present actionable business insights and recommendations to support Q2 planning.

## 4. Project Scope

| Area | Scope |
|---|---|
| Geographic Coverage | 10 Tier-2 Cities in India |
| Analysis Period | January–June 2026 (H1 2026) |
| Data Sources | CSV and MySQL |
| Data Analysis | SQL-based business analysis |
| Visualization | Microsoft Power BI |
| Deliverables | SQL Reports, Power BI Dashboard, Documentation, and Business Presentation |

## 5. Expected Deliverables

- **SQL Business Analysis:** Reusable SQL queries addressing key operational and passenger-related business questions.
- **Power BI Dashboard:** An interactive, self-explanatory dashboard for monitoring business performance across cities.
- **Business Insights:** Data-driven findings highlighting performance gaps, passenger behaviour, and growth opportunities.
- **Recommendations:** Actionable recommendations to improve trip performance, passenger retention, and marketing budget allocation.
- **Project Documentation:** Technical documentation and a presentation summarizing the analysis and recommendations.

## 6. Business Outcome

Provide Goodcabs with a clear understanding of its H1 2026 performance, identify the key drivers of business performance across cities, and support data-driven decisions for Q2 marketing and operational planning.

The validated POC will serve as the analytical foundation for future integration with Goodcabs' live data pipeline.
